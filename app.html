Inbox & Drive Cleaner

Clear out the emails you never read, and move big Drive files to an account that still has free space.

Gmail account

1. Find who fills your inbox

Reads the sender of your most recent emails and counts them. Tick the ones you want gone, then add them as a rule.

Uses the sender's own unsubscribe option, like Gmail's Unsubscribe button. 1-click and email happen right here; web page opens the sender's page in a background tab for you to finish. Only unsubscribe from senders you recognize: for spam, it confirms your address is real (just trash those).

2. Your rules

Emails matching any rule that is switched on get cleaned. Each rule shows the exact Gmail search it runs, so you can paste it into Gmail to check.

3. Run

4. Automatic cleanup

Runs your switched-on rules by itself and moves the matches to Trash. Chrome needs to be open; if it was closed at that time, the cleanup runs next time you open it.

Accounts

Files are copied by Google from one account to the other (nothing is downloaded to this computer), checked, then removed from the full account.

    Connect to Google (one time)

    The extension talks to Gmail and Drive with your own Google Cloud OAuth client, so your data only goes between your browser and Google. Full steps are in the README; in short:

    1. In Google Cloud Console, create a project, then enable the Gmail API and the Google Drive API.
    2. Open Google Auth Platform and click Get started: app name, your email, audience External, your email again, agree, Create.
    3. Left menu Audience → Test users → Add users: add every Google account you'll connect.
